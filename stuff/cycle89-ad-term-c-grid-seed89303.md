# Cycle 89: Anderson-Darling term c grid, independent seed (member-3)

Second, independent run of the cycle 89 grid. Different seed and generator from
`cycle89-ad-term-c-grid.md` (numpy PCG64 here, Python `random` there).

## Definition

With z the sample-standardized order statistics (mean, sd with ddof=1) of a sample of size n,

t_i = -((2i-1)/n) * [ln Phi(z_(i)) + ln(1 - Phi(z_(n+1-i)))],  i = 1..n.

Statistic: S_c = sum_i log(1 + c t_i)/c for c > 0, and S_0 = sum_i t_i.
Reject when S_c exceeds its 95% quantile under N(0,1) at the same n (normal-calibrated, 5% size).
Gain = power(c) - power(c=0) on the same samples; paired SE = sd(1[S_c rej] - 1[S_0 rej]) / sqrt(20000).

Seed 89303 (numpy `default_rng`, one stream drawn in order n, then normal, t(3), t(5), cn).
Sim count: 20000 per n per law. cn = 0.9 N(0,1) + 0.1 N(0,9).

## Results

Per n: which c maximizes t(3) gain, monotonicity over 0.25..3, and t(5)/cn at the t(3)-optimal c.

| n | t(3)-best c | t(3) gain (SE) | gain monotone over 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 3? | t(5) gain at best c (SE) | cn gain at best c (SE) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | 2 | +0.0793 (0.0022) | no: turns at c=3 (+0.0758) | +0.0646 (0.0021), > 2 SE gain | +0.0825 (0.0022), > 2 SE gain |
| 50 | 3 | +0.1190 (0.0025) | yes (c=2 +0.1187, c=3 +0.1190) | +0.1389 (0.0027), > 2 SE gain | +0.1539 (0.0027), > 2 SE gain |
| 100 | 2 | +0.0912 (0.0020) | no: turns at c=3 (+0.0907) | +0.2113 (0.0030), > 2 SE gain | +0.1643 (0.0027), > 2 SE gain |

First n and c where the t(3) gain turns: n=25, c=3. At every n the gain falls from c=3 to
c=5 to c=10, and c=10 still gains over c=0 by more than 20 SE.

The t(3)-optimal c gains under t(5) and under the contaminated normal by more than two
paired SE at every n. It never loses.

c=2 and c=3 are within 0.004 of each other at every n. The difference at n=50 (0.0003)
is far below one SE. That SE is for the gain over c=0, not the c=2 vs c=3 difference,
so it is only a rough reference.

## Verdict

Fix c = 2. It maximizes t(3) gain at n=25 and n=100, and is within 0.0003 of the maximum at n=50.
It gains under t(5) and under 0.9 N(0,1) + 0.1 N(0,9) by more than two paired SE at every n.
