Flip at n=400: SW never leads beyond two paired SE at any shape tested. Both tests saturate at gamma(2) and gamma(5), gamma(10) is a tie (-0.9 SE), and the first S2 lead beyond two paired SE is gamma(20) (sqrt(beta1) = 0.447, -0.0249, -16.0 SE). Reading: the crossover does not move monotonically in sqrt(beta1). At n=50 SW leads everywhere down to sqrt(beta1) = 0.28 and the lead merely fades to noise below 0.2. By n=100 the ordering has inverted at moderate skew: S2 leads from sqrt(beta1) = 0.63 down and SW only wins at 0.89 and above. The 0.63 to 0.89 bracket is stable from n=100 to n=200. At n=400 the bracket appears to drop to 0.45 to 0.63, but that is because both tests are at or near power 1 from gamma(10) up, so there is no skew left at which SW can show a lead; where power is not saturated (gamma(20), gamma(50)) S2 leads by a growing margin (-2.5 and -8.2 points). The pattern is: SW's edge is a small-n phenomenon in the 0.3 to 0.9 skew range, and S2's edge in the mild-skew range widens with n (gamma(50): -2.7 points at n=100, -6.5 at n=200, -8.2 at n=400).
