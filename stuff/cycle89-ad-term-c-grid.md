# Cycle 89: best c below 20 on the Anderson-Darling term scale

Definition. For a sample of size n, standardize by the sample mean and sample sd (n-1), sort to get z_(1) <= ... <= z_(n), and set the Anderson-Darling term t_i = -((2i-1)/n) * [ln Phi(z_(i)) + ln(1 - Phi(z_(n+1-i)))]. The statistic is S_c = sum_i log(1 + c t_i)/c for c > 0 and S_0 = sum_i t_i. Rejection is at the 5% size calibrated on the normal law: the critical value for each (c, n) is the 95th percentile of S_c over the 20000 normal sims at that n, and the same 20000 sims serve as the normal row. Power is the fraction of sims exceeding that critical value. Gain over c=0 is the mean of the paired rejection difference 1[S_c rejects] - 1[S_0 rejects] on the same samples, with SE the sd of that difference over sqrt(20000).
