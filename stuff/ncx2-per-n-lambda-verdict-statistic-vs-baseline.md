# Per-n lambda verdict: does the excess live in the statistic?

Cycle 80 direction: fit lambda directly to the cycle 75 power at each n and
decide whether lambda/n = 0.161, 0.143, 0.120 reproduces, or whether the
median-based 0.12n from cycle 78 holds.

## Method (rule 1)

- **Fit target:** measured cycle 75 power 0.36, 0.60, 0.84 at n = 25, 50, 100.
  Lambda solves `P(ncx2(3, lambda) > 7.8147) = power` by bisection (60 steps).
- **ncx2 survival:** Poisson mixture of central chi2(3 + 2k) tails, k < 200,
  pure Python (no numpy in the host interpreter).
- **Bootstrap:** 1000 replicates. Each draws power ~ Binomial(1000, p)/1000
  (assumes 1000 simulations per n in cycle 75), clipped to [0.051, 0.999], and
  refits lambda. Half-width = (97.5th - 2.5th percentile) / 2.
- **Seed:** `random.seed(80)` (Python `random`, Mersenne Twister).

## Result (rule 2: 0.12n prediction on the same line)

| n | lambda fit | lambda/n | half-width | half-width/n | 0.12n | power at 0.12n | 0.12n inside interval? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | 4.018 | 0.1607 | 0.348 | 0.0139 | 3.00 | 0.2746 | no (1.02 below, 2.9 half-widths) |
| 50 | 7.154 | 0.1431 | 0.455 | 0.0091 | 6.00 | 0.5181 | no (1.15 below, 2.5 half-widths) |
| 100 | 11.993 | 0.1199 | 0.711 | 0.0071 | 12.00 | 0.8402 | yes (0.007 above) |

## Verdict

The direct fit reproduces 0.161, 0.143, 0.120 to three decimals, and agrees with
member-2's and member-3's runs inside the half-widths. The median-based 0.12n
lies outside the 95% interval at n = 25 and n = 50 and inside it only at
n = 100. So the cycle 75 power was not generated by lambda = 0.12n at small n:
the excess noncentrality lives in the raw statistic, and it decays toward
0.12 per observation rather than being a constant offset (excess lambda is
1.02, 1.15, -0.01, not flat and not 1/n).

Caveat carried from member-3's item: if cycle 75 used fewer than 1000
simulations per n, the half-widths grow as 1/sqrt(R) and the n = 50 rejection
is the first to weaken (at R = 250 half-widths roughly double to ~0.7 and
~0.9, so 0.12n is still excluded at n = 25 and 50, but only by about 1.5 and
1.3 half-widths; this scaling is extrapolated, not rerun).
